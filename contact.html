Frequently Asked Questions
You can reset your password anytime via the Maybank Online Banking login page. Click Forgot Password, verify your identity using your registered mobile number or email, and follow the on-screen instructions. For security, you will receive a One-Time Password (OTP) to complete the process.
Log in to Maybank Online Banking or the Maybank app, navigate to Cards & Accounts > Manage Cards, select the card you wish to activate, and tap Activate Card. Alternatively, you can activate your card at any Maybank ATM by performing a PIN-based transaction.
DuitNow is Malaysia's instant fund transfer service. You can send and receive money using a DuitNow ID — which can be your mobile number, NRIC, or business registration number — instead of a bank account number. Access it via Maybank Online Banking or the app under Transfer > DuitNow.
You can apply online through the Maybank website, via the mobile app, or at any Maybank branch. Eligibility requirements include a minimum annual income of RM 24,000. You'll need your MyKad, latest 3 months' salary slips, and EPF statement. Approval is typically within 5 to 7 working days.
